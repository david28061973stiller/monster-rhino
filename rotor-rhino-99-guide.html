Your final fitting.
Three steps to finish.

Your motor and electronics are already installed and calibrated. Start at the equivalent of Step 11 in the full guide: fit the lever tube, connect its grip cable as described in full-guide Step 9, and refit the cover. The steps below are numbered 1–3 for this delivery option.

External USB cable, XT60 power cable and 24 V power supply are not included. Info & prices ↗

Before you begin

Disconnect power and USB before opening the enclosure or connecting the grip cable. Recalibrate the axis if you change the physical throw limiter; see the official VPforce Configurator manual.

STEP 01 / FULL GUIDE 11

Install and adjust the lever tube

Open the clamp and position the carbon tube. The angled grip connector should point upward for ergonomics. You can loosen the clamp and rotate the tube to adjust the grip angle to your preferred ergonomic position, then retighten the clamp firmly by hand. Do not overtighten. Adjust the effective lever length by choosing the cable outlet opening used at the holder. Route the grip cable as shown without trapping it in the clamp; fit the clamp half and tighten the screws evenly and firmly by hand. Do not overtighten. With the power supply and USB disconnected, remove the electronics cover to access the mainboard. Connect the preinstalled grip connector cable to the GRIP connection shown in the official VPforce wiring diagram, following Step 9 of the full guide. Check connector orientation and pin alignment before insertion. Do not repeat the motor installation or initial calibration.

Additional cable openings can be made with a handheld rotary tool. Match the dimensions of the existing openings. Seal exposed cut edges with thin cyanoacrylate adhesive to help prevent delamination, and allow it to cure before reassembly.

CAUTION — CARBON FIBRE DUST & SPLINTERS

Machining carbon fibre can release dust and sharp fragments that are hazardous to health. Avoid inhalation and contact with skin or eyes. Remove the tube from the assembly before machining. Work outdoors away from other people or use effective local dust extraction; outdoor work does not replace suitable respiratory, eye and skin protection.

Prevent dust from spreading to clothing, the motor and electronics. Carefully remove all chips and dust from inside and outside the tube and clean the work area before reinstalling the tube. Use suitable filtered extraction or damp cleaning; do not blow residues away with compressed air or dry-brush them into the air.

STEP 02 / FULL GUIDE 12

Fit the electronics cover

Arrange the wiring inside the enclosure. Use the supplied Wiring utilities bag, containing cable sleeving and cable ties, to bundle the wires. Leave sufficient slack for lever movement and guide the sleeved cables through the cover exits. Fit the cover with its four corner screws without trapping wires.

STEP 03 / FULL GUIDE 13

Assembly complete

Remove the protective cap from the grip connector thread before fitting your grip. This cap is supplied only to protect the thread against transport damage. Compare your assembly with the reference photo. Check the fasteners and cable clearance throughout the intended travel. Alternative throw limiter STL files for local 3D printing are available directly from Winger on request. Available angles: 20°, 25°, 30°, 35° and 40°. Recalibrate the axis whenever you install a different throw limiter.

To request the alternative throw limiter STL files, contact Winger on Discord: srf_winger_73_david, or email wingers.support@gmail.com. Please specify the angle(s) you need.

Allow sufficient clearance for the full lever travel, your grip and the optional counterweight box.
