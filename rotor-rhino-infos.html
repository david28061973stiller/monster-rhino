POWER / PURCHASE SEPARATELY

Choose your power supply.

Rotor Rhino requires an external regulated 24 V DC power supply, purchased separately. VPforce recommends 8 A for a single-axis kit.

A Mean Well LRS-350-24 supplies 24 V at 14.6 A (350.4 W). We do not recommend it as a shared supply for simultaneous Monster Rhino and Rotor Rhino operation at their full recommended power budgets. VPforce recommends 15 A for a dual-axis kit and 8 A for a single-axis kit: approximately 23 A / 552 W combined, before reserve.

One supply for both

Mean Well RSP-750-24

24 V DC · 31.3 A · 751.2 W. Provides capacity above the combined 23 A planning budget. This is a power-sizing recommendation, not a tested plug-and-play dual-device wiring kit.

Use two separately protected DC branches with correctly rated wiring, connectors and DC switching. Each device needs its own appropriately configured current limit. Do not route the combined supply current through the Monster Rhino E-Stop.

Mean Well RSP-750-24 — manufacturer datasheet ↗

Separate supplies

Monster Rhino: LRS-350-24

Keep the existing 24 V / 14.6 A supply and the current limits specified for your Monster Rhino configuration.

Rotor Rhino: LRS-200-24

24 V DC · 8.8 A · 211.2 W. Meets VPforce’s 24 V / 8 A single-axis recommendation at the datasheet’s applicable operating conditions. Observe temperature and mounting derating; size any additional accessories separately.

Keep the DC outputs separate; do not parallel the two power supplies.

INSTALLATION & CURRENT LIMITS

These chassis power supplies require safe mains installation, protective earthing, suitable enclosure and ventilation by a qualified person. Observe the manufacturer’s instructions and derating curves. The higher available supply current does not increase the permitted current of cables, connectors or E-Stops.

The standard Monster Rhino E-Stop is limited to 9 A as specified in its existing safety instructions. Retain that limit unless the switch and complete relevant circuit are upgraded appropriately. The Configurator PSU current setting concerns DC bus current; do not confuse it with the 30 A motor phase-current limit.
