IMPORTANT — DISCONNECT POWER WHEN NOT IN USE

Rotor Rhino has no built-in on/off switch or emergency stop. Always disconnect its motor power supply when the collective is not in use. For example, switch off the power supply at a suitable switched power strip, or unplug it from the mains.

Disconnecting USB or closing the simulator does not disconnect motor power. Keep the power-disconnection point accessible. A switched power strip is a means of switching off the power supply; it is not an emergency stop.

If Monster Rhino and Rotor Rhino share one power supply, switching it off disconnects both devices. The Monster Rhino emergency stop does not automatically disconnect power to Rotor Rhino.

STEP 10 / 3 mm hex

Firmware, calibration & lever connector

No built-in power switch or emergency stop. Keep the supply disconnection point accessible, and always disconnect motor power after use. Read the power safety notice ↑

Connect the electronics for setup and update the firmware through the official VPforce website. First establish the desired gearbox output centre position at a RAW value of approximately 2000, manually rotating the output shaft as required. Fit the lever connector in its centre position, observing the stop pin position shown. Secure its clamping screw with a 3 mm hex key. Then calibrate the axis in the VPforce software against the travel limits of the installed physical throw limiter. This final axis calibration defines the usable travel; it is separate from establishing the initial RAW centre position. Refer to the official Configurator manual for software operation.

STEP 11

Install and adjust the lever tube

Open the clamp and position the carbon tube. The angled grip connector should point upward for ergonomics. You can loosen the clamp and rotate the tube to adjust the grip angle to your preferred ergonomic position, then retighten the clamp firmly by hand. Do not overtighten. Adjust the effective lever length by choosing the cable outlet opening used at the holder. Route the grip cable as shown without trapping it in the clamp; fit the clamp half and tighten the screws evenly and firmly by hand. Do not overtighten. Connect the grip cable according to the VPforce wiring diagram.

Additional cable openings can be made with a handheld rotary tool. Match the dimensions of the existing openings. Seal exposed cut edges with thin cyanoacrylate adhesive to help prevent delamination, and allow it to cure before reassembly.

CAUTION — CARBON FIBRE DUST & SPLINTERS

Machining carbon fibre can release dust and sharp fragments that are hazardous to health. Avoid inhalation and contact with skin or eyes. Remove the tube from the assembly before machining. Work outdoors away from other people or use effective local dust extraction; outdoor work does not replace suitable respiratory, eye and skin protection.

Prevent dust from spreading to clothing, the motor and electronics. Carefully remove all chips and dust from inside and outside the tube and clean the work area before reinstalling the tube. Use suitable filtered extraction or damp cleaning; do not blow residues away with compressed air or dry-brush them into the air.

03 / COUNTERWEIGHT GUIDANCE

Thermal headroom.
Optional fine-tuning.

The counterweight box is included. Use it to fine-tune balance and reduce motor holding effort if desired.

Motor load & balance

For the standard 425 mm lever setup with a 350 g SharKa-50 grip and a 10:1 gearbox, the simplified static motor load is estimated at approximately 0.11 Nm before transmission losses. This suggests that exact mechanical balance may not be necessary for normal operation; optional counterweights can reduce the continuous holding effort.

This is general orientation for the example configuration, not a guaranteed continuous load or temperature rating. Grip weight, moving parts, effects, settings and cooling affect the actual load.

Optional counterweight adjustment

Use the included box to fine-tune balance and reduce motor holding effort if desired. Additional weights are purchased separately. Small self-adhesive wheel-balancing weights allow gradual adjustment.

If excessive heating or thermal power reduction occurs, reduce the load and check settings and cooling first. Gradually adding counterweight can further reduce holding effort. Adjust with power disconnected and the lever supported; secure weights and check clearance over the full travel.

Counterweights do not guarantee protection against overheating. Follow VPforce operating limits and provide adequate cooling.
